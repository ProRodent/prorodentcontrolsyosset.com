Hamlet, village or borough: is it important?

An unincorporated community with no government of its own, sitting inside a town, is what the word hamlet means. That matters a little for paperwork and not at all for rodents.

Custom, postal service and census use define hamlets more than any legal boundary does. We rely on the ZIP code because that is what the coverage list uses.
