What federal guidance says about cleanup.

Come back here every time pellets, urine stains or bedding scraps turn up.

  • Sweeping, blowing and vacuuming are all off the table, since airborne dust is how germs travel.
  • A space that has been shut up needs its doors and windows opened, and then you step away for half an hour.
  • Consider each soiled surface contaminated, and keep your eyes and mouth clear of gloved hands.
  • Pick up the wet waste with paper towels and no dry rags, and spray again whenever anything looks dusty.
  • One tied bag per session keeps the waste together and the routine simple.
  • If illness strikes within a few weeks, bring up the rodent contact with your doctor.
