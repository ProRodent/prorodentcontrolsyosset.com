East and South Farmingdale are different places.

Both share the 11735 code, so a Farmingdale mailing address does not always point to the village. The practical side stays the same: a call, a survey of the attic and basement, then a priced plan.

First things people notice.

Most conversations open with a remark very much like one of these.

Storms, frost and thaw.

Fallen leaves pile up along walls and fences and give rodents cover. Rake them away from the foundation and the routes between yard and house become harder to use.

Safer handling while you wait.

Read the whole list before lifting anything, because a calm minute now protects your lungs.

  • Sweeping, blowing and vacuuming are all off the table, since airborne dust is how germs travel.
  • Plan on thirty minutes of ventilation before any cleaning begins.
  • Peel the gloves off inside out, bag disposable ones, then scrub your hands.
  • Soak droppings and urine stains with a household disinfectant or a bleach dilution and let the liquid sit roughly five minutes.
  • A sealed bag keeps stray particles from trailing you through the house.
  • Feeling off afterward? Tell a doctor what the cleanup involved and let the doctor judge.
