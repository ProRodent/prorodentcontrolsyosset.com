Great Neck: a peninsula area, not one municipality.

Our coverage data lists four ZIP codes for it: 11022, 11020, 11021 and 11024, at 6.6 to 12.5 miles from Syosset. CDC method: wet it down first.

Slow movement keeps grit away from your face.

  • Even a soft dust mop flings old pellets upward, so no dry tool starts the job.
  • Airing out a closed room for about thirty minutes is the first CDC move.
  • Thin disposable gloves suit small messes, while heavier rubber pairs suit big ones and can be rinsed.
  • Pick up the wet waste with paper towels and no dry rags, and spray again whenever anything looks dusty.
  • Use a second bag if the first one is thin or heavy.
  • If you fall ill after contact with droppings, that is a medical question, and the clinician needs to know what you handled.
