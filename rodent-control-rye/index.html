Why October matters.

October nights on Long Island are chilly and outdoor food is scarce. Rodents begin hunting for a warm spot, and garages, attics and crawl spaces top the list. Their search keeps going until the spring thaw.

What homeowners mention first.

What follows are the sights, sounds and smells that most often lead to a first call.

  • Pellets scattered over a shed or cellar shelf.
  • A screen or vent cover with a hole punched in it.
  • A parked car with a musty odor or chewed wiring.
  • Droppings beneath the porch or on deck boards.
