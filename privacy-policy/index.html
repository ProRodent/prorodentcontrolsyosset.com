How it works

What reaches us.

The site has no forms or sign-up boxes, so its pages never collect your name or email address. Information arrives only when you phone or write, and it is limited to what you choose to share, such as an address, a callback number and a sentence about the trouble.

What we do with it.

Your contact details are used to schedule the visit, perform the work, follow up afterward and answer questions. We do not sell anything you tell us.

Cookies and outside services.

These pages are static and set no cookies of their own. The embedded map and the web fonts come from outside providers. Those providers can see technical data such as your IP address, and their own policies govern it.

Asking us about it.

Send privacy questions to contact@prorodentcontrolsyosset.com. The policy may change from time to time, and the newest version always replaces this one.
