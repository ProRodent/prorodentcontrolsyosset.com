Putting the pieces in order.

Most jobs follow one order. The inspection comes first, naming the animal and attaching figures to the work. Next, trapping removes what is inside and sealing shuts the entries. When the house calls for it, cleanup or repair finishes things.

Buying the whole sequence is never required. If a replaced vent cover or a traced smell is all you want, say so and the estimate lists only that.

Is there a separate page for emergencies?

No, and that is deliberate. Urgent help appears on every page. Someone answers at all hours, and a same-day visit goes out whenever there is space.

Where do I begin?

When in doubt, book the inspection first, since it identifies the animal and sets out each job in sequence with separate prices.

Can I buy one piece alone?

Yes, any of the four jobs can be booked by itself, though sealing ahead of repair spares you from doing the repair twice.

Do you take business customers?

Yes. Loading doors, shared walls, dumpsters and the contents of the brief post-visit report are all explained on the commercial page.
