Hamlet, village or borough: is it important?

Hamlet houses are not special by virtue of the name. The differences lie in the lot, the building's age and the way it gets used.

An unincorporated community with no government of its own, sitting inside a town, is what the word hamlet means. That matters a little for paperwork and not at all for rodents.

How a job is staged.

Our tech surveys, sets devices, closes the holes and comes back to confirm. The order is not negotiable, because sealing too early can lock an animal inside the house. Afterward you get a written list of what was found and what we advise, with a separate price beside each item.
