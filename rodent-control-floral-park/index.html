Suburban lots and rodent cover.

Trash cans with loose lids teach rodents to stop by every week. A latch or bungee cord costs next to nothing and breaks the habit.

A woodpile resting on the ground against a wall works like a hotel. Move it to a rack some feet from the house and the fuel stays while the guests do not.

Dust control before the visit.

The federal pages are summarized loosely here, and a doctor, not a pest company, should answer any health question.

  • Even a soft dust mop flings old pellets upward, so no dry tool starts the job.
  • Garages, attics and sheds trap stale air, so give them thirty minutes of fresh flow before you step in.
  • Consider each soiled surface contaminated, and keep your eyes and mouth clear of gloved hands.
  • Wet the ground beneath and around the waste too, since urine reaches further than the stain suggests.
  • A sealed bag keeps stray particles from trailing you through the house.
