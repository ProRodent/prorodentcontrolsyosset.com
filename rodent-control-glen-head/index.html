Cleanup rules from public health agencies.

Slow movement keeps grit away from your face.

  • Sweeping, blowing and vacuuming are all off the table, since airborne dust is how germs travel.
  • Plan on thirty minutes of ventilation before any cleaning begins.
  • Consider each soiled surface contaminated, and keep your eyes and mouth clear of gloved hands.
  • Disinfectant needs time to work, so wait several minutes after spraying before wiping.
  • Put soiled towels, any dead animal, and the gloves or tongs you used into one plastic bag, knot it and set it in the household trash.
  • Our skill is pest work, which means sickness belongs with a health professional.
