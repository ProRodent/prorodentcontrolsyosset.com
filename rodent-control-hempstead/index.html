The Village of Hempstead, not the Town.

Hempstead here means the incorporated Village of Hempstead, formed in 1853, and it is a different thing from the much larger Town of Hempstead around it. Our coverage data lists ZIP codes 11549 and 11550, at 8.5 and 9.7 miles from Syosset. The takeaway for a resident is to phone about droppings or gnawing, since the technician surveys each house on its own terms.

When the thermometer drops.

October nights on Long Island are chilly and outdoor food is scarce. Rodents begin hunting for a warm spot, and garages, attics and crawl spaces top the list. Their search keeps going until the spring thaw.

Do this meanwhile.

  • Tonight, pour kibble and wild bird seed into containers with snap lids.
  • Lift the water bowl at bedtime and dry the counters.
  • Resist setting traps of your own, since stray ones scramble the trail we have to read.
  • Note the date you first caught a sound, a smear or a smell.

Dust control before the visit.

Public health sources agree on most points, so the shared ground is boiled down below.

  • Wet first and move second: the spray bottle comes out before the dustpan.
  • Airing out a closed room for about thirty minutes is the first CDC move.
  • Thin disposable gloves suit small messes, while heavier rubber pairs suit big ones and can be rinsed.
  • On a shelf dotted with pellets, work from the outer edges inward so none skitter off.
  • Knot the bag before carrying it, and walk it straight to the outdoor bin.
  • Our skill is pest work, which means sickness belongs with a health professional.
