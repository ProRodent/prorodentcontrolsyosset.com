A hamlet that never became a village.

Uniondale once saw an attempt at incorporation fail, and it remains unincorporated. For you, the result is that the Town of Hempstead handles local rules. The practical side stays the same: a call, a survey of the attic and basement, then a priced plan.

Storms, frost and thaw.

Soaking rain can push burrowing rats toward higher ground, including the space beneath a deck or shed. When the water drains away, search for fresh holes and shifted soil.

Early warnings worth acting on.

Some of these clues have other explanations, but every one is worth a phone call.

  • Seed hulls under the feeder at sunrise.
  • A fresh hole next to the air conditioner pad.
  • Chewed foam on a cable running outdoors.
  • Pellets in a seldom-used kitchen drawer.

Small steps ahead of the visit.

  • Choose a single room to keep shut overnight.
  • Test whether the dryer vent flap swings closed and the garage door sits flush.
  • Let the dispatcher know if anyone at home has asthma or breathing trouble.
  • Keep your phone audible, since the technician may call before arriving.

What federal guidance says about cleanup.

Following this routine keeps airborne dust low during the wait.

  • Even a soft dust mop flings old pellets upward, so no dry tool starts the job.
  • A storeroom that stayed closed for months should not be entered cold.
  • Put gloves on at the beginning, and close out the job with soap and water.
  • On a shelf dotted with pellets, work from the outer edges inward so none skitter off.
  • Regular trash pickup takes the bag, and any reusable tool gets rinsed.
  • Deciding whether symptoms trace back to a cleanup is for a clinician, so get examined if you feel unwell.
