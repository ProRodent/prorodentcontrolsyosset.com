What each season does.

Winter never stops rodents. It herds them inside, where attic fluff, cartons and still corners serve as nest sites. A house shut up for the season gives them months of peace.

If your lot is near a bay or canal.

Many waterfront lots end up with logs, kelp and snarled limbs after a storm. Piles against siding hide gaps and shelter animals, so haul them away promptly.

Low sandy ground drains quickly and digs easily. After heavy rain a burrow beside the foundation can shift, which is why the tech looks for fresh soil instead of old holes.
