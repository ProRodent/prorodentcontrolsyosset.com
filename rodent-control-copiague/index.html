If your lot is near a bay or canal.

Salt air rusts things silently. From the ground a vent screen can look intact while its rim has rotted away to a gap as wide as a pencil. Coated or stainless mesh outlasts plain steel.

Crawl spaces and low basements on flood-prone lots collect damp and odor, and a dead animal there can go unnoticed for weeks. Look in after every flood.

Before you touch a single pellet.

Slow movement keeps grit away from your face.

  • Even a soft dust mop flings old pellets upward, so no dry tool starts the job.
  • Make a cross-draft, wait roughly half an hour, then enter.
  • At the end, wash with soap and running water, even though gloves stayed on.
  • Disinfectant needs time to work, so wait several minutes after spraying before wiping.
  • Regular trash pickup takes the bag, and any reusable tool gets rinsed.
  • Feeling off afterward? Tell a doctor what the cleanup involved and let the doctor judge.
