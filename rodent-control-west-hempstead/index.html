Dust control before the visit.

Dry droppings and nest scraps can carry germs. Slow, damp work is the safe route, and the CDC pages linked at the end support each step.

  • Sweeping, blowing and vacuuming are all off the table, since airborne dust is how germs travel.
  • Make a cross-draft, wait roughly half an hour, then enter.
  • Put gloves on at the beginning, and close out the job with soap and water.
  • Wet the ground beneath and around the waste too, since urine reaches further than the stain suggests.
  • A carcass gets lifted with a gloved hand or a shovel, never with bare skin.
  • Our skill is pest work, which means sickness belongs with a health professional.
