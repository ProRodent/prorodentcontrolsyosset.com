Treat the mileage as a rough guide, and phone to confirm if the exact distance matters to you, since the person who answers can judge a borderline street far better than a table can.

Range and ZIP questions.

Why is there no row for Syosset in the table?

Our coverage file lists the 51 places shown and has no ZIP row for Syosset itself. Addresses in the hamlet are booked by street, so give yours and the visit is confirmed during the call.

My town is listed but I live at the edge of it. Does that matter?

No. Any ZIP code shown here is covered. Distance is measured from the center of each ZIP area, so addresses at the fringe are judged on the phone.

Two places share a name. Which one do you mean?

Look at the type column. Bellerose, for example, appears here as a Queens neighborhood, and Hempstead appears as a village, not the larger town around it.

Is the Berry Hill Road address an office?

No. It exists so directories can list us. No one is stationed there, and each visit happens at the customer's own property.
