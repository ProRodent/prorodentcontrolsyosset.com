Before you touch a single pellet.

Public health sources agree on most points, so the shared ground is boiled down below.

  • Wet first and move second: the spray bottle comes out before the dustpan.
  • Plan on thirty minutes of ventilation before any cleaning begins.
  • Put gloves on at the beginning, and close out the job with soap and water.
  • Pick up the wet waste with paper towels and no dry rags, and spray again whenever anything looks dusty.
  • A sealed bag keeps stray particles from trailing you through the house.
  • If you fall ill after contact with droppings, that is a medical question, and the clinician needs to know what you handled.
