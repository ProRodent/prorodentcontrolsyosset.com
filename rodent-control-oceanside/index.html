Low-lying lots and what they mean.

A property beside a bay, canal or marsh meets a few South Shore realities. Salt air eats plain steel screens, low ground floods and storm wrack stacked against a wall gives cover. All of these are quick to check.

Salt air rusts things silently. From the ground a vent screen can look intact while its rim has rotted away to a gap as wide as a pencil. Coated or stainless mesh outlasts plain steel.
