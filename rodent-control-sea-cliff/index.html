What being near the water changes.

Waterside lots frequently gather logs, kelp and snarled limbs once a storm passes. Piles against siding hide gaps and shelter animals. Clear them quickly and, once the water has gone down, look for fresh burrows.

Near salt air, rust works quietly. A vent screen that looks solid from the ground may have corroded at its edges and left a gap the width of a pencil. Coated or stainless mesh holds up better.

Safer handling while you wait.

Following this routine keeps airborne dust low during the wait.

  • Wet first and move second: the spray bottle comes out before the dustpan.
  • Airing out a closed room for about thirty minutes is the first CDC move.
  • Because fingers drift to the face, gloves stay on until the last bag is knotted shut.
  • Wet the ground beneath and around the waste too, since urine reaches further than the stain suggests.
  • A carcass gets lifted with a gloved hand or a shovel, never with bare skin.
  • If you fall ill after contact with droppings, that is a medical question, and the clinician needs to know what you handled.
