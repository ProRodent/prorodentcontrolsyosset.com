A protective checklist for droppings.

The federal pages are summarized loosely here, and a doctor, not a pest company, should answer any health question.

  • Sweeping, blowing and vacuuming are all off the table, since airborne dust is how germs travel.
  • A space that has been shut up needs its doors and windows opened, and then you step away for half an hour.
  • Skin should never touch pellets, urine spots or shredded nest fluff.
  • Disinfectant needs time to work, so wait several minutes after spraying before wiping.
  • Knot the bag before carrying it, and walk it straight to the outdoor bin.
  • Keep an eye on your health for several weeks, and if flu-like symptoms or breathlessness appear, see a doctor.
