Safer handling while you wait.

Dry droppings and nest scraps can carry germs. Slow, damp work is the safe route, and the CDC pages linked at the end support each step.

  • Sweeping, blowing and vacuuming are all off the table, since airborne dust is how germs travel.
  • A storeroom that stayed closed for months should not be entered cold.
  • Peel the gloves off inside out, bag disposable ones, then scrub your hands.
  • For the home mix, the CDC calls for bleach and water at a ratio of 1 to 9, prepared new for every session.
  • One tied bag per session keeps the waste together and the routine simple.
