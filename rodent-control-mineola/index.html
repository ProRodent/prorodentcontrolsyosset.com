Mineola, the Nassau County seat.

Mineola is an incorporated village, formed in 1906, and the county seat of Nassau County. It lies mainly in the Town of North Hempstead, with a small southern part in the Town of Hempstead. ZIP code 11501 sits 8.6 miles from Syosset. Whether a house is old or new, we inspect the same entry points and the same insulation.

Being the county seat is a fact about civic life, not about rodents. The work follows the same method as elsewhere.

Small clues behind big worries.

  • The dog digging at a single patch of lawn.

Hamlet, village or borough: is it important?

In a village or a hamlet, a mouse still needs only a pencil-wide gap. The label is background.

Mature plantings beside the house.

Large trees tend to lean across a roof, and a limb that rubs the shingles serves as a bridge. Pruning it counts among the cheapest rodent jobs available.

A band of bare soil or gravel next to the foundation strips away cover. It costs little, works well and helps later repairs last.

Cleanup rules from public health agencies.

This box restates public health advice line by line, and it stays useful whether or not you book a visit.

  • A leaf blower aimed into a dusty garage corner is the worst idea, with a household vacuum a close second.
  • Plan on thirty minutes of ventilation before any cleaning begins.
  • At the end, wash with soap and running water, even though gloves stayed on.
  • Damp material releases less dust, so soaking comes before any wiping.
  • Put soiled towels, any dead animal, and the gloves or tongs you used into one plastic bag, knot it and set it in the household trash.
  • Keep an eye on your health for several weeks, and if flu-like symptoms or breathlessness appear, see a doctor.
